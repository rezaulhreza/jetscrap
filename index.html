Pause and settings

On a phone or tablet

Left thumb
Touch anywhere on the left half and slide. Left and right run; up jumps and fires the jetpack; down drops through platforms
Right thumb
Touch anywhere on the right half and slide to aim. Slide to the outer ring to shoot
Buttons
Grenade, Reload, Swap (gun or pistol) and Pick up, on the right edge
II and Scores
Pause, and show the scoreboard

Health, armour, ammunition, grenades and power-ups are collected by walking over them. Overdrive gives 50% more damage; Haste gives speed and unlimited fuel. Both last 10 seconds.
